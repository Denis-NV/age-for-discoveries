POST CAPTION (English):
The cross on every Portuguese caravel during the Age of Discovery? That's the Templar cross — or rather, the cross of the Order of Christ, which is what the Templars became when Portugal refused to disband them. Portugal lobbied the Pope and in 1319 an identical order rose from the same soil, the same people, the same headquarters in Tomar. Prince Henry the Navigator became Grand Master in 1420 and put the Order's wealth into ships.

The Charola at the heart of this complex was modelled on the Church of the Holy Sepulchre in Jerusalem, built in the 12th century by those same knights. The Manueline stonework outside came later, paid for by the riches those caravels brought back. They went to sea under the sign of the cross. They came back and carved the sea into stone.

Have you been to Tomar?
